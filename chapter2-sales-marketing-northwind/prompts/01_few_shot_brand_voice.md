# Prompt 1 — Few-Shot Brand-Voice Matching (section 2.3)

```
Match the voice of these two examples of existing Northwind copy:

Example 1 (landing page): "Most project tools ask you to change how
your team works. Northwind doesn't. It fits around the way you
already run things — then gets out of the way."

Example 2 (nurture email): "You set up your first project three days
ago. No pressure — just wanted to flag that most teams see the most
value once they've invited a second teammate. Worth trying before your
trial ends?"

Now write a cold outreach email introducing Northwind to an operations
manager at a 40-person logistics company, in this same voice.
```

Compare the result against the two examples in `brand-voice/examples.md`
— short sentences, no hype language, directness. If your output reads
like generic SaaS marketing copy, the voice-matching didn't take;
try again with the examples pasted in full, not summarized.
