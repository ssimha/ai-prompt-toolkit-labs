# Customer Segment Profiles

Use these with prompts/02_segment_specific.md.

## Segment: Startup (1-15 employees)

- Primary concern: speed of setup, low learning curve, price sensitivity
- Typical buyer: founder or first ops hire, often the same person using it day to day
- What they've usually done by day 3 of trial: created 1-2 projects, invited 0-1 teammates
- Feature they care least about: granular permissions (team is small, trust is high)

## Segment: Mid-Market (16-99 employees)

- Primary concern: cross-team visibility, integrations with existing tools (Slack, Salesforce)
- Typical buyer: an operations manager or a director evaluating for multiple teams
- What they've usually done by day 3 of trial: created 2-3 projects, invited 1-3 teammates
- Feature they care most about: integrations and reporting dashboards

## Segment: Enterprise (100+ employees)

- Primary concern: permissions, audit trails, SSO, procurement/security review
- Typical buyer: an operations director, often with IT/security in the approval loop
- What they've usually done by day 3 of trial: created 1 project, invited 0 teammates
  (enterprise trials often stall waiting on security sign-off)
- Feature they care most about: SSO, audit trail, granular permissions
