# Northwind Brand Voice — Reference Examples

Use these as the few-shot examples in prompts/01_few_shot_brand_voice.md.
Do not paraphrase them into a tone description ("friendly, confident") —
paste them verbatim into prompts, the same way section 2.3 describes.

## Example 1 (landing page hero copy)

> Most project tools ask you to change how your team works. Northwind
> doesn't. It fits around the way you already run things — then gets
> out of the way.

## Example 2 (trial nurture email, day 3)

> You set up your first project three days ago. No pressure — just
> wanted to flag that most teams see the most value once they've
> invited a second teammate. Worth trying before your trial ends?

## What to notice in both

- Short sentences, low word count per sentence
- Confident but not hype-driven — no exclamation points, no "game-changing"
- Directness — gets to the point in the first sentence
- A light, slightly informal register ("No pressure", "gets out of the way")
